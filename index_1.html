01Who we are

A culinary collective.
A meeting place.

The Agency was founded on a simple belief - that food is the universal language, and that the best things happen when the right people meet over a table. We exist to create those meetings.

We represent chefs whose work deserves a global audience. We represent brands ready to grow with intention. And we produce the activations between them - the partnerships, projects, and press moments that move our industry forward.

Rooted in Dubai, drawing on Arabic and Mediterranean traditions of hospitality, we work across the GCC, Europe and beyond. Local in soul. International in reach.

"The best things happen when the right people meet over a table."

06Our approach

We don't sell taste.
We connect it.

I.

The right match.

A great activation starts with a great fit. We pair chefs and brands by values and chemistry first - the deal terms come after the trust does.

II.

Story over spectacle.

Trends fade. Narrative compounds. Every press moment, partnership, and activation is a chapter in a longer book - we build careers and brands that endure.

III.

Rooted, not regional.

We're proudly anchored in Dubai, drawing on Arabic and Mediterranean traditions of hospitality - but we represent globally. Heritage is a launchpad, never a ceiling.

07How we produce

A five-act story.

Concept to comms. We own all five acts - so chefs can cook and brands can host, and neither has to think about logistics, press lists, or what happens at 11pm when the third wave arrives.

Concept

- 02

Hospitality

- 05

Comms

Capture, press, post. The activation that travels - beyond the room, into the feed and the headlines.
